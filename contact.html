Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help me recover lost funds?

No. We cannot help with lost funds. Our tools are 100% client-side, which means we never see your private keys, seed phrases, or any sensitive information. You are solely responsible for securing your own assets.

Do you provide customer support for the tools?

We provide community support through GitHub issues and Twitter. For urgent matters, you can email us. Please understand that as a free, open-source project, we may not be able to respond immediately to all inquiries.

Can I contribute to the project?

Yes! NexaPay is open source. You can contribute code, report bugs, suggest features, or improve documentation on our GitHub repository.

Are you hiring or looking for partnerships?

We're always open to discussing partnerships with like-minded projects. Email us with partnership proposals.

⚠️ Important Notice

We cannot help with:

  • Lost private keys or seed phrases
  • Recovering stolen funds
  • Transaction issues or failed transfers
  • Investment advice or trading decisions

Our tools are educational and provided "as is." You are responsible for your own security and decisions.
