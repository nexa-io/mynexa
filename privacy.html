Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2025

Nexa ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website, services, and platform (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and other information you provide (e.g., phone number, address).
  • KYC Information: For verification purposes, we collect identification documents (e.g., passport, driver’s license) through third-party providers.
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed by third-party providers (e.g., Paystack) and not stored by us.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about your interactions with the Service, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience, such as remembering login sessions or preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service, including account management and contract tracking.
  • Process payments for trading challenges.
  • Verify your identity for KYC and AML compliance.
  • Send notifications about your account, contracts, or Service updates.
  • Analyze usage to improve the Service and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: For KYC verification (e.g., Docuseal), payment processing (e.g., Paystack), or analytics.
  • Legal Authorities: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal requests.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store your data in secure environments, including localStorage for non-sensitive data and third-party servers for KYC information. We implement:

  • Encryption for data transmission (e.g., HTTPS).
  • Access controls to limit unauthorized access.
  • Regular security assessments to protect against breaches.

However, no method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location (e.g., EU for GDPR, California for CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing for marketing or other purposes.

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@nexa.com. We will respond within 30 days (or as required by law).

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions (e.g., authToken in localStorage).
  • Track usage for analytics and performance improvements.
  • Personalize your experience on the Service.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may affect functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services, including:

  • Paystack: For payment processing.
  • Docuseal: For contract signing and KYC verification.
  • Cloudflare: For hosting and content delivery.

These providers have their own privacy policies, which you should review.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service (e.g., active account or contracts).
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax or AML records).
  • Resolve disputes or enforce agreements.

Data is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed, unless required by law.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your jurisdiction, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses for GDPR) for international transfers.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child’s data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date. Continued use of the Service constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

12. Contact Us

For questions or to exercise your data protection rights, contact us at:

  • Email: support@nexa.com
  • Website: Contact Support

By using Nexa, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
