Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

🔒 No Tracking · No Data Collection · Client-Side Only

At NexaPay, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle (or more accurately, don't handle) your personal information when you use our tools.

1. The Short Version

We don't collect, store, or process any of your personal data. All our tools run 100% in your browser. We have no servers that receive your data, no databases that store your information, and no analytics that track your usage.

2. What Data We DO NOT Collect

❌ We DO NOT collect:

  • Personal Information: Names, emails, phone numbers, addresses
  • Financial Information: Wallet addresses, transaction history, balances
  • Sensitive Data: Private keys, seed phrases, passwords
  • Usage Data: Which tools you use, when you use them, how often
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser information, device details
  • Location Data: Your geographic location or timezone

3. How Our Tools Work (Technically)

🔧 Technical Implementation:

Our website consists of:

  • Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files
  • No backend servers or databases
  • No user accounts or login systems
  • No cookies (except essential session cookies if using GitHub Pages)
  • No third-party analytics or tracking scripts

When you use our Wallet Generator:

  • JavaScript runs in YOUR browser
  • Keys are generated locally using YOUR device's resources
  • Encryption happens locally with YOUR password
  • Files are downloaded to YOUR computer
  • Nothing is sent to our servers

4. Server Logs

Our website is hosted on static hosting services (like GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages). These services may collect standard server logs for operational purposes. We have no control over these logs and do not access or use them.

We intentionally:

  • Disable any optional logging features
  • Do not analyze server logs
  • Do not correlate log data with user activity

5. Third-Party Services

We use minimal third-party services:

  • Fonts: Google Fonts (loaded from Google's servers)
  • Hosting: Static file hosting (GitHub Pages/Cloudflare Pages)
  • No other third-party services (no analytics, no tracking, no ads)

6. Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking. If our hosting provider sets essential cookies (like session cookies), these are for technical functionality only and are not used by us for any purpose.

7. Children's Privacy

Our tools are not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store your data, there's no risk of data breaches from our servers. However:

  • You are responsible for securing your own devices
  • You should use strong, unique passwords
  • You should keep your encrypted wallet files secure
  • You should use antivirus and security software

9. Your Rights

Under data protection laws (like Nigeria's NDPR), you have rights including:

  • Right to Access: You can ask what data we have about you (answer: none)
  • Right to Deletion: You can ask us to delete your data (answer: we have none to delete)
  • Right to Object: You can object to data processing (answer: we don't process data)

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Since we don't collect data, changes will typically only reflect technical improvements or legal requirements.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Email: infocontactnexa@gmail.com
  • Twitter: @NexaPay

🔐 Our Privacy Promise

We are committed to maintaining our "no data collection" approach. As we add new features, we will:

  • Always default to client-side processing
  • Never add tracking without clear notice
  • Keep all code open source for transparency
  • Respect user privacy above all else
