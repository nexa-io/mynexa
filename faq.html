Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nexa?

A prop trading firm in Nigeria offering 1-Step (10% profit, 6% drawdown) and 2-Step (8% profit/phase, 10% drawdown) challenges to access funded accounts with 80/20 (demo) or 160/20 (live) profit splits.

How do I join?

Sign up, accept T&C/Privacy Policy, sign the Trading Agreement (link), and complete KYC (link).

What are the rules?

No leverage limits; banned strategies: martingale, news scalping, high-frequency, grid, copy trading manipulation; one account per Trader; video submission (max 5 min) required.

When can I request payouts?

30 days after funded status, if KYC verified. Min $3, max 10% account size, every 14 days thereafter.

What is KYC?

Submit NIN slip and selfie via form. Verified via NIMC QR scan.

What if I breach rules?

Account terminated, no refunds, possible EFCC referral for fraud.

Is Nexa compliant?

Yes, follows Nigerian AML/CTF laws with KYC and audit logs.
