Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nexa?

Nexa is a platform that helps traders get funded by completing trading challenges, allowing you to trade with control and keep a share of your profits.

How do I get started with Nexa?

Sign up with your name, email, and password, then choose a challenge (Micro, Starter, Standard, or Pro) to begin trading.

What are Nexa challenges?

Challenges are evaluations where you trade a funded account to meet profit targets within a set timeframe to qualify for funding.

How much do challenges cost?

Challenge prices are: $2 for a $100 Micro account, $3.5 for a $250 Starter account, $7 for a $500 Standard account, and $13 for a $1000 Pro account.

What happens when i immediately i purchase an account?

Important Trading Account Instructions Submit Trading Account ID: After purchasing an account or completing a phase, you must submit your Trading Account ID via the dashboard before starting to trade. Failure to do so will require you to restart the process to meet this requirement. Move to the Next Phase: Once you clear a phase, use the "Move to Next Phase" button in the dashboard sidebar to proceed. Your progress will be reviewed, and you’ll receive all updates directly on your dashboard. Note: The dashboard sidebar contains two buttons: Submit Trading ID: To submit your Trading Account ID. Move to Next Phase: To advance after clearing a phase. Stay updated via your dashboard for all notifications and next steps.

What is the difference between one-step and two-step challenges?

One-step challenges require meeting a single profit target, while two-step challenges have two phases with different targets for flexibility.

Can I choose my trading platform?

Yes, you can use any trading platform you prefer, as long as your strategies comply with Nexa’s rules.

What is the KYC process?

After passing a challenge, you submit identity documents securely online to verify your identity before receiving funding.

How long does it take to get funded?

Once you meet profit targets and complete KYC, funding is typically processed within a few business days.

What happens if I pass a challenge?

You’ll submit KYC, sign a contract, and receive a funded account to trade, sharing profits with Nexa.

What are the profit-sharing terms?

Traders keep a substantial share of profits (typically 70-80%) from funded accounts, based on challenge terms.

Can I trade multiple accounts at once?

No, Nexa allows you to trade only one account at a time to ensure focus and compliance with challenge rules.

What if I fail a challenge?

If you don’t meet the profit targets, you can repurchase the same or a different challenge without penalties.

Is there a time limit for challenges?

Yes, challenges have specific timeframes (e.g., 30 days for one-step, 60 days for two-step) to meet profit targets.

Do I need trading experience to join Nexa?

No, traders of all levels can join, but some experience is recommended to succeed in challenges.

What trading strategies are allowed?

Legitimate strategies like scalping or swing trading are allowed, but strategies meant to cheat or exploit the system are strictly prohibited.

How does Nexa ensure fair trading?

Nexa enforces clear profit targets and rules, prohibiting cheating or exploitative strategies to maintain fairness.

What is the admin panel for?

The admin panel allows our team to manage contracts, update KYC statuses, and send notifications to support traders.

Can I track my challenge progress?

Yes, your dashboard displays your contract details, including account size, status, and KYC progress, updated in real-time.

How do I receive notifications?

Notifications about your account, KYC, or contracts appear in your dashboard after logging in.
