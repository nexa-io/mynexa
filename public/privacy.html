Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2025

Nexa ("we," "us," or "our"), a company incorporated under the laws of Seychelles and operating in Nigeria, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our website, services, and platform (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the practices described in this policy, in compliance with Nigeria’s Nigerian Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and Seychelles’ Data Protection Act.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and other information you provide (e.g., phone number, address).
  • KYC Information: For verification purposes, we collect identification documents (e.g., passport, driver’s license) through third-party providers.
  • Payment Information: Payment details are processed by third-party providers (e.g., Paystack) and not stored by us.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about your interactions with the Service, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience, such as remembering login sessions or preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the Service, including account management and contract tracking.
  • Process payments for trading challenges.
  • Verify your identity for KYC and AML compliance under Nigerian and Seychelles regulations.
  • Send notifications about your account, contracts, or Service updates.
  • Analyze usage to improve the Service and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations, including NDPR and Seychelles’ Data Protection Act.

3. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: For KYC verification (e.g., Docuseal), payment processing (e.g., Paystack), or analytics.
  • Legal Authorities: To comply with applicable laws, such as Nigeria’s Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) requirements or Seychelles’ Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

We store your data in secure environments, including localStorage for non-sensitive data and third-party servers for KYC information. We implement:

  • Encryption for data transmission (e.g., HTTPS).
  • Access controls to limit unauthorized access.
  • Regular security assessments to protect against breaches.

However, no method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We comply with NDPR and Seychelles’ Data Protection Act security requirements.

5. Your Data Protection Rights

Under Nigeria’s NDPR and Seychelles’ Data Protection Act, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection: Object to processing for marketing or other purposes.

For users in the EU, additional GDPR rights apply. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@nexa.com. We will respond within 30 days, as required by NDPR and Seychelles law.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions (e.g., authToken in localStorage).

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings, but disabling cookies may affect functionality. We comply with NDPR guidelines on cookie consent.

We retain your data for as long as necessary to:

Data is deleted or anonymized when no longer needed, unless required by Nigerian or Seychelles law.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Nigeria and Seychelles, including the United States. We ensure appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses for GDPR or NDPR-compliant agreements, for international transfers.
