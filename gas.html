Understanding Gas Fees

Gas fees are payments made by users to compensate for the computing energy required to process transactions on blockchain networks.

Computational Cost

Gas fees pay for the computational work of executing smart contracts and transactions.

Network Demand

Fees increase when many users are transacting simultaneously (network congestion).

Transaction Speed

Higher gas fees prioritize your transaction for faster inclusion in the next block.
