Simple Tips

💡

What are gas fees?

Fees you pay to send transactions on blockchain networks. Like paying for postage.

⏰

When to send?

Weekends and late nights (UTC time) are usually cheaper than weekday afternoons.

🚀

Need it fast?

If you want your transaction to be processed quickly, you'll need to pay higher fees.

🔄

Save money

Use networks like Base, Arbitrum, or Polygon for much lower fees than Ethereum.

Quick Questions

What's a "standard transaction"?

A simple transfer of tokens from one wallet to another. More complex actions (like trading) cost more.

Why do fees change?

When many people are sending transactions at the same time, fees go up. When it's quiet, fees go down.

What's Gwei?

It's a unit for measuring gas fees. Just like cents are for dollars. Lower Gwei = cheaper transaction.

Can I avoid fees?

You can't avoid fees completely, but you can save by choosing cheaper networks or waiting for quiet times.
